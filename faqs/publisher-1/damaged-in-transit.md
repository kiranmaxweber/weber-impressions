# Damaged in transit

It happens, and it is not your problem to solve.

Photograph the outer carton before you unwrap any further, then photograph the book. Send
both. We'll send a replacement from stock the same day where we have one, and refund in full
where we don't.

Don't return the damaged copy unless we ask. Most of the time we'd rather you kept it or
passed it on than paid postage to send a damaged book back to us.
