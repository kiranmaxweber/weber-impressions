# Returns and refunds

Return anything within 14 days of delivery, for any reason, provided it comes back unread
and in its original wrap. Tell us first — returns sent without notice are hard to match to
an order.

You pay return postage unless the book arrived damaged or we sent the wrong title. We refund
to the original card within five working days of the book reaching us.

Signed and numbered copies are not returnable unless they arrive damaged.
