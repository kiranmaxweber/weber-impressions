# Shipping

We ship same day on orders placed before 3pm, Monday to Friday. Orders placed after that go
out the next working day.

Everything travels tracked. You'll have a number within an hour of the parcel leaving.

- United Kingdom — 1 to 2 working days
- Europe — 3 to 5 working days
- United States and Canada — 5 to 8 working days
- Rest of world — 7 to 14 working days

Transit times are the carrier's estimate, not a guarantee. Customs can add a week to any
international parcel and we have no visibility of it while it sits there.
