# Taxes, duties, and customs

US sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable.

Books shipping from outside the United States may attract import duty or a customs handling
fee on arrival. These are charged by the carrier or the customs authority, not by us, and
they are the recipient's to pay. We can't predict them and we can't pay them on your behalf.

We declare the full value on every parcel. We will not mark an order as a gift or understate
its value.
