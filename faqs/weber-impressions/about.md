# About Weber Impressions

Weber Impressions is a storefront for independent photobook publishers. We don't print
books. We hold a small amount of stock from presses we admire, take orders on their behalf,
and get the books to you.

Each publisher sets its own terms — shipping, returns, editions. We tell you whose book
you're buying before you buy it, and we say so again on the order. Where a publisher's
policy applies, that policy governs.

Our booksellers read every title we carry. If you want a recommendation, ask for one.
