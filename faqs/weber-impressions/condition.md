# Condition

Our books are new unless a listing says otherwise.

Photobooks are made in short runs and handled by hand. Slight shelf wear to a dust jacket,
a soft corner, or a faint mark on a slipcase is common on new stock and is not a defect.
Where wear is more than slight, we photograph it and say so in the listing.

If a book arrives in a condition the listing didn't prepare you for, tell us. Photograph it
before you unwrap it further.
