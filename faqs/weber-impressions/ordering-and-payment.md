# Ordering and payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Apple Pay. Cards are authorised at
checkout and charged when the book ships.

An order containing books from more than one publisher ships in more than one parcel, from
more than one place, on more than one schedule. You are charged once per parcel as it
leaves. Nothing is charged for a book that never ships.

Prices are in US dollars. We do not offer instalments, layaway, or trade accounts.
