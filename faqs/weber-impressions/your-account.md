# Your account

An account keeps your order history, addresses, and any notes you have left for us. You can
place an order without one.

To change the delivery address on an order that hasn't shipped, ask and we'll pass it to the
publisher. Once a parcel is with a carrier, the address is theirs to change and usually
can't be.

To close an account, ask. We'll remove it and confirm.

To reset your password, use the link on the sign-in page. We'll email you; the link is good
for an hour. We can't see your password and won't set one for you.
