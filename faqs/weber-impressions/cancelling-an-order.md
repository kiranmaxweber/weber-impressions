# Cancelling an order

A book that hasn't shipped can be cancelled outright. Nothing has been charged, so there is
nothing to refund, and no fee applies.

Once a book has shipped, cancelling is no longer possible and the publisher's returns policy
applies instead. Those policies differ. Check the one for the publisher in question.

Because our publishers ship on their own schedules, one book in an order may be cancellable
while another is already in transit.
